This demo runs on fictitious data. In production the page is served by Google Apps Script and reads the master table every time it opens. To try the loader, choose a CSV with the columns country, user, date, task, items_assigned, items_done, uploaded, not_uploaded (for example data/sample_main_data.csv), or paste copied cells. The data stays in your browser.
